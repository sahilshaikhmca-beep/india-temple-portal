Char Dham / Chota Char Dham

Badrinath Temple

📍 Badrinath, Uttarakhand

Set beside the Alaknanda river, it is part of both the Char Dham and Chota Char Dham circuits.

DeityVishnu (Badri Narayan)
Era / HistoryAncient; traditionally associated with Adi Shankaracharya
Best time to visitMay to June and September to October (closed in winter)

Highlights

  • Tapt Kund hot springs
  • Mana village
  • Alaknanda river
