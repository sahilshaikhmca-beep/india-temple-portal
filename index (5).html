Dravidian Architecture

Meenakshi Amman Temple

📍 Madurai, Tamil Nadu

A vast Dravidian temple complex known for its towering gopurams covered in colourful sculptures.

DeityMeenakshi (Parvati) and Sundareswarar (Shiva)
Era / HistoryPresent complex largely built during the Nayak period (16th-17th century)
Best time to visitOctober to March

Highlights

  • 14 gopurams
  • Hall of Thousand Pillars
  • Golden Lotus Tank
