Char Dham / Jyotirlinga

Ramanathaswamy Temple

📍 Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu

Famous for its long pillared corridors and 22 holy water tanks (theerthams) inside the complex.

DeityShiva
Era / HistoryPresent structure built mainly between the 12th and 17th centuries
Best time to visitOctober to April

Highlights

  • Longest corridors among Indian temples
  • 22 theerthams
