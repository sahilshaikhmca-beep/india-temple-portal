Brihadisvara Temple

📍 Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu

A masterpiece of Chola architecture, famous for its 66 m vimana and a monolithic granite capstone.

DeityShiva
Era / HistoryCompleted around 1010 CE by Rajaraja I
Best time to visitOctober to March

Highlights

  • Vimana tower
  • Chola frescoes
  • Large Nandi monolith
