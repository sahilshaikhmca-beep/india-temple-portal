Jyotirlinga

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

📍 Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, set on the banks of the Ganga in one of the world's oldest living cities.

DeityShiva
Era / HistoryPresent structure rebuilt in 1780 by Ahilyabai Holkar
Best time to visitOctober to March
