Pilgrimage Circuits

Char Dham (Four Abodes)

The four great pilgrimage sites at the four corners of India, traditionally linked to Adi Shankaracharya.

⏱ 2-4 weeks by road and rail

  1. Badrinath (North)
  2. Dwarka (West)
  3. Puri (East)
  4. Rameswaram (South)

Chota Char Dham (Uttarakhand)

A Himalayan circuit open only in the warmer months.

⏱ 10-12 days

  1. Yamunotri
  2. Gangotri
  3. Kedarnath
  4. Badrinath

Twelve Jyotirlingas

Twelve major Shiva shrines across India.

⏱ Planned over several trips

  1. Somnath
  2. Mallikarjuna
  3. Mahakaleshwar
  4. Omkareshwar
  5. Kedarnath
  6. Bhimashankar
  7. Kashi Vishwanath
  8. Trimbakeshwar
  9. Vaidyanath
  10. Nageshwar
  11. Rameshwaram
  12. Grishneshwar
