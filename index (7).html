Jyotirlinga / Chota Char Dham

Kedarnath Temple

📍 Kedarnath, Uttarakhand

A high-altitude Himalayan shrine near the source of the Mandakini river.

DeityShiva
Era / HistoryAncient; the present structure is traditionally linked to Adi Shankaracharya
Best time to visitMay to June and September to October (closed in winter)

Highlights

  • Himalayan trek from Gaurikund
  • Chorabari Tal
  • Bhairavnath Temple
