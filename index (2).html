Konark Sun Temple

📍 Konark, Odisha

A temple designed as a colossal chariot of the Sun God, with intricately carved stone wheels.

DeitySurya
Era / History13th century, attributed to Narasimhadeva I
Best time to visitOctober to February

Highlights
