About this project

This portal presents structured information about Indian temples and pilgrimage destinations in a simple, easy-to-use interface, to improve access to heritage and pilgrimage information.

Built with Python and Flask. Temple data lives in data.py and is easy to extend. Always confirm timings, dates and travel conditions with official sources before you travel.

JSON API: /api/temples
